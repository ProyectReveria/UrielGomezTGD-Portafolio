# Narrative Design Documents

Welcome to the Narrative Design section of my portfolio!

I love creating stories, and narrative design is a major part of my work. For example, approximately 60% of the *Chronostasis: Reveria* Game Design Document focuses on its story and narrative design.

My portfolio includes documentation for active game projects, books, shared narrative universes, and experimental concepts developed over several years.

**This section is divided into two main categories:**

- **Narrative Design Documents:** Documentation for selected projects, including game narratives, worldbuilding, lore, storylines, and supporting materials such as level schematics, scripts, and questlines.
- **Concepts Never to See EOAF (End of Active Development):** Selected concepts and older projects that are no longer in active development, require rewriting, or have been superseded by newer narrative approaches. Some of their ideas and lore remain part of my broader narrative universe.

I have been writing since 2018, and this portfolio represents only a selection of my work. Some projects are still in development, others are intended for future continuation, and some are included because they demonstrate my narrative design process and the evolution of my ideas.

For those unfamiliar with my documentation system, I work in both English and Spanish. Documents may be available in either language or both, depending on the project. Unless otherwise specified, available language versions are included in the linked documents or PDFs.

**Note:** Some documents also contain level schematics, scripts, questlines, and other game design materials.

# **Shared Narrative Universe**

*Chronostasis: Reveria*, *Voiceless Flower*, and *Broken Leven Heart* are part of a shared universe spanning multiple books and video game projects. Although not all books are currently included in this repository, and game development has been a lengthy process that has only recently resumed and Restart, they all contribute to the same overarching narrative.

The *Chronostasis* storyline comprises at least seven books, including *The City of Ashes*, *Elden Dark Agartha*, *The Golden Door of Gold and Silver*, *Roses and Tears*, and *Fire Rain*, among others. *Chronostasis: Reveria* represents the culmination of this storyline.

*Voiceless Flower* takes place before the events that conclude this narrative, while *Broken Leven Heart* continues the shared universe through a different storyline following the events of *Reveria*.

For further information about the universe and its narrative connections, please feel free to ask. The *Broken Leven Heart* and *Chronostasis: Reveria* Game Design Documents are available in their respective sections of this repository.

# Concepts Never to See EOAF (End of Active Development)

Throughout my work on large-scale narratives, I have developed concepts with unique interactions, compelling ideas, and interesting narrative possibilities. However, I have not always found a way to continue developing them.

These concepts range from dystopian worlds and stories within the *Chronostasis* saga to more dramatic and philosophical narratives. This section presents selected examples of my work, including concepts that were discontinued, projects that require rewriting, and stories whose ideas remain relevant to my broader narrative universe.

**Notes:**
- Many of the links below are migrations from OneDrive to Google Docs.
- Some books are available only in Spanish. English translations were produced primarily for portfolio purposes.
- Not all books are included here. Some are older projects dating back to 2018, several of which underwent four to five years of development between 2018 and 2023. Other, more recent works, developed from 2024 onward, are significantly more complex and require extensive research through my OneDrive archives to document properly.

## Aegleeker

**Status:** Original concept — 2018

*Aegleeker* was part of the original *Chronostasis* storyline. The project was eventually discontinued due to its complexity and the development of new narrative approaches, particularly those related to Sarin as a character.

However, much of its material remains relevant to the broader universe, including events and lore that extend beyond the beginning of *Broken Leven Heart*. The newer narrative replaces *Aegleeker* while preserving or reworking elements of its original ideas.
